How this actually works

Better targeting, not a promised number

Most underperforming ad campaigns aren't underperforming because of the platform — they're underperforming because the ad is written to get clicks, not to filter for people who are actually ready to book.

Every ad we build is written and targeted around your specific service and the patient who's already close to deciding — filtering for procedure interest and readiness before someone ever reaches your front desk, instead of optimizing for the cheapest possible click.

We don't promise a specific number of new patients or a fixed return — every practice, market, and season is different, and anyone who guarantees an exact outcome in advance isn't being straight with you. What we commit to is building every ad around the mechanism above, and showing you plainly whether it's working.

Questions practices actually ask

FAQ

What's the catch?

None that we're aware of. The first ad has no transaction attached to it, so there's nothing to walk back. If you don't want to continue after seeing it, you don't have to — you still keep the ad.

Do I have to sign anything to get the free ad?

No. There's no contract, form, or call required to receive it. It's built and sent to you directly.

Will this look like generic AI content?

Every ad is built around your practice's actual services, location, and positioning — not a stock template swapped with your logo. You'll see the finished creative before any conversation about continuing.

How is patient data handled?

Ad targeting is built around service and procedure interest, not individually identifying patient health information. We take a compliance-aware approach to how campaign data is handled and are happy to walk through specifics for your practice.

I've been burned by a marketing agency before. How is this different?

Most agencies ask for budget before proving anything. We reverse that order — you see a finished, working ad first, and the conversation about paying for more only happens after that.

What if it doesn't work?

Then you keep the free ad and we've cost you nothing but a look. Our responsibility ends at delivery — we build the ad, you run it. How it performs depends on your targeting, follow-up, market, and offer, which are outside our scope.

Is there a contract if I want to continue?

Month-to-month. No annual commitment, no cancellation fee.

How is pricing structured?

The first ad is free. If you choose to continue, pricing is based on a monthly batch of custom ads rather than a single static campaign — we'll walk through exact numbers for your practice directly.

Which platforms do the ads run on?

Typically Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google, depending on where your specific patient search behavior concentrates — we'll tell you which we recommend and why for your practice.

How is this different from boosting a Facebook post?

A boosted post optimizes for engagement. These ads are built and targeted specifically to filter for people close to booking a specific procedure — a different goal, with different creative and targeting behind it.
